## Scholarly Apparatus Note {#scholarly-apparatus-note}

The notes prioritize consequential empirical, current, technical, documentary, and disputed claims. Author-created frameworks are identified as normative syntheses rather than externally validated instruments. Organizational and practitioner cases are cited as reports of their own practice unless independently studied. Live mission statistics are dated and methodologically qualified.
