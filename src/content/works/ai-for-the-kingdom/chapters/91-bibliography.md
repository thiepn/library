## Artificial Intelligence

Because AI changes rapidly, readers should consult current primary documentation and recent research in addition to general works. Useful source categories include:

- peer-reviewed machine-learning research;
- official documentation from model and platform providers;
- standards and risk-management guidance from national and international technical bodies;
- current research on AI safety, privacy, bias, multilingual systems, and human-computer interaction;
- current evaluation literature on machine translation, speech technology, and low-resource language modeling.

## Mission Data and Current Statistics

Mission statistics change continually and vary by methodology. For current figures, consult the latest public materials from organizations specializing in:

- global people-group research;
- Bible translation progress;
- language vitality and classification;
- migration and demographic research;
- persecution and religious-freedom reporting;
- mission-agency field research.

Readers should always note the date, definition, and methodology behind numerical claims.
