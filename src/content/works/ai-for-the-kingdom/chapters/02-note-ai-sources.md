Artificial intelligence systems are discussed in this book primarily at the level of practical capability and Christian stewardship. The field changes rapidly, so specific products, benchmarks, model names, or technical limits may become outdated.

Readers should verify time-sensitive technical claims against current documentation and primary sources. AI-generated outputs should never be treated as authoritative merely because they are fluent or confident.

Where this book discusses Scripture, theology, mission practice, language, or historical claims, readers are encouraged to consult the biblical text and reliable primary or scholarly sources rather than relying on generated summaries.
