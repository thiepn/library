Artificial intelligence was used during the development of this book. AI-assisted workflows contributed to research discovery, structural analysis, drafting, comparison, revision, source organization, and editorial review. AI-generated material was not treated as evidence. Empirical, historical, theological, statistical, and documentary claims were traced to identifiable sources before inclusion, and organizational self-reports are treated as such rather than as independent proof.

Artificial intelligence changes quickly. Current statistics, technical capabilities, legal developments, and product-specific information were checked through the final research period in August 2026. Where a claim is likely to change materially, the text identifies its date or methodology rather than presenting the information as timeless.

The author remains responsible for the arguments, judgments, interpretations, errors, and final published text.
