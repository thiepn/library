The double command introduced in Chapter 3 now returns with full force. Love for God moves into relationships, material responsibility, church life, witness, and mission. The first four chapters focus mainly on life within communities and relationships: neighbor and enemy, harm and repair, vocation and mercy, authority and safety. The final two move outward into explicit witness and cross-cultural mission. In every case, love seeks the true good rather than mere approval, refuses coercion, and recognizes that responsibility is real but differentiated.
