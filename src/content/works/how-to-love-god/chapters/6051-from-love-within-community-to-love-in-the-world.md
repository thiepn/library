Love does not stop at the boundaries of family, congregation, or familiar community. The same Christ who forms a people also sends that people as witnesses. Yet outward movement must carry forward everything learned in the preceding chapters: truth without coercion, mercy without leverage, authority without domination, differentiated responsibility, respect for conscience, and concern for the vulnerable.

Christian witness therefore cannot be reduced to private example, but neither can mission be reduced to aggressive persuasion or institutional expansion. The gospel has words that must be spoken. Disciple-making belongs to the church's continuing vocation. Some believers are called and sent in more specialized ways, including across cultures. The next two chapters distinguish these layers so that every Christian can participate without pretending every Christian has the same missionary role.
