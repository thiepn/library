The following resources are grouped for reader exploration rather than used as a hidden authority list. Inclusion does not imply agreement with every position in a work. Complete publication details appear in the **Recommended Reading Bibliography** in Appendix L.

## Loving God, theology, and Christian formation

- Augustine, *Confessions*.
- Bernard of Clairvaux, *On Loving God*.
- John Owen, *Communion with God*.
- Jonathan Edwards, *Religious Affections*.
- J. I. Packer, *Knowing God*.
- Michael Reeves, *Delighting in the Trinity*.
- Jerry Bridges, *The Discipline of Grace*.
- Dietrich Bonhoeffer, *Life Together*.
- John Piper, *When I Don't Desire God*.

## Prayer, suffering, and pastoral experience

- C. S. Lewis, *A Grief Observed*.
- D. Martyn Lloyd-Jones, *Spiritual Depression*.
- D. A. Carson, *How Long, O Lord?*
- Federico G. Villanueva, *Psalms 1–72: A Pastoral and Contextual Commentary*.
- Yohanna Katanacho, *Praying Through the Psalms*.

## Church, culture, and global Christian theology

- Hwa Yung, *Mangoes or Bananas? The Quest for an Authentic Asian Christian Theology*.
- Simon Chan, *Grassroots Asian Theology*.
- Mercy Amba Oduyoye, *Hearing and Knowing: Theological Reflections on Christianity in Africa*.
- Kwame Bediako, *Christianity in Africa*.
- Lamin Sanneh, *Translating the Message*.

## Mission and the global church

- David J. Bosch, *Transforming Mission*.
- Lesslie Newbigin, *The Gospel in a Pluralist Society*.
- Lesslie Newbigin, *The Open Secret*.
- Christopher J. H. Wright, *The Mission of God's People*.
- Samuel Escobar, *The New Global Mission*.
- Jehu J. Hanciles, *Migration and the Making of Global Christianity*.
- Richard Twiss, *Rescuing the Gospel from the Cowboys*.

## Spiritual warfare and contextual discernment

- Opoku Onyinah, *Pentecostal Exorcism: Witchcraft and Demonology in Ghana*.

## Church, authority, and safeguarding

- Diane Langberg, *Redeeming Power: Understanding Authority and Abuse in the Church*.
- Basyle Tchividjian and Shira M. Berkovits, *The Child Safeguarding Policy Guide for Churches and Ministries*.
- GRACE—Godly Response to Abuse in the Christian Environment, safeguarding and abuse-response resources.

### Scope note

These resources are primarily North American and do not replace current local law, denominational safeguarding procedures, emergency services, or qualified local professional advice. Reporting duties, mandatory-reporting rules, investigation structures, confidentiality requirements, and protective processes vary by jurisdiction.

Readers should use recommended resources as conversation partners under Scripture. Historical works may contain assumptions or arguments that require contextual evaluation; modern works likewise reflect particular traditions and settings.
