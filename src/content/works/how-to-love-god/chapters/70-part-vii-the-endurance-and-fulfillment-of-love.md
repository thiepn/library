Christian love is not measured only at beginnings. Early zeal can be real and still need deepening. Seasons change. Bodies age. Churches disappoint. Callings shift. Believers may pass through migration, family responsibility, persecution, success, obscurity, grief, and long stretches of quiet faithfulness. The final chapters ask how love continues and where it is going. Grace sustains imperfect believers toward resurrection and perfected communion rather than flawless intensity in this life.
