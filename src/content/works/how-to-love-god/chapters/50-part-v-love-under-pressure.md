The previous part described the normal patterns of Christian formation. This part asks what happens when faithfulness comes under pressure—when desire is low, suffering persists, rivals become powerful, or spiritual opposition is feared. No single explanation fits every hardship. Scripture gives a richer realism: sin, bodily weakness, grief, social harm, temptation, the world, and demonic opposition are all real, but they are not interchangeable.
