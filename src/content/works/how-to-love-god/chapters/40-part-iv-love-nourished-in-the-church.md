Christian formation is often described as though the individual believer were the primary unit and the church were one helpful resource among many. The New Testament gives a different picture. God saves people into a people. His Word is heard in community as well as read privately. Prayer is personal and corporate. Baptism and the Lord's Supper belong to gathered Christian life. Gifts are given for mutual service. Repentance, encouragement, correction, generosity, and worship are practiced together. This part explores that shared ecology without romanticizing churches or human authority.
