The first part ended with a problem. The command is comprehensive and beautiful, but sinners do not simply need better techniques for carrying it out. Grace must renew the person. Part II therefore asks what is wrong with us, what God gives in new life, on what basis believers are accepted, and what kind of communion follows. These chapters are not a chronological ladder of salvation. They are a theological cluster: realities Scripture joins closely even where Protestant traditions differ on the precise order in which some should be described.
