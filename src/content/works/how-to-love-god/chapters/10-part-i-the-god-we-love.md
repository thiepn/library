Before we ask how love grows, we need to know whom we are called to love and what the word *love* is doing. Christian devotion does not begin with an undefined spiritual feeling. It begins with the God who has made himself known. The first four chapters move from God's identity and worth, to the meaning of wholehearted love, to Jesus' joining of God-love and neighbor-love, and finally to the grace that makes Christian love possible. The sequence keeps technique in its proper place: we do not begin by manipulating the heart but by attending to God, his command, and his prior action.
