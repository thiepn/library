Grace does not leave love undefined. The Godward life takes shape. It obeys, desires, repents, fights sin, and grows. These realities are easily pulled apart. Some Christians fear that serious obedience is legalism; others make obedience the whole of love. Some make delight the test of authenticity; others distrust feeling altogether. Some treat grace as passivity; others turn disciplines into spiritual technology. These chapters hold the pieces together.
