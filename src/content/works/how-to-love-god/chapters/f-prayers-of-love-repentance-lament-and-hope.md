These prayers are original prompts shaped by biblical themes. They are not Scripture and need not be repeated word for word.

## Adoration and love

Father, you are not the God of my imagination but the living God who has made yourself known. Teach me to receive what is true about you, including what corrects me. Let truth become worship rather than pride. Through your Son, draw my desires, trust, reverence, and obedience toward you. By your Spirit, make my love sincere and patient. Keep every good gift in its proper place beneath you.

## Receiving grace

God of mercy, I bring you no performance that can purchase acceptance. Thank you for Christ, for his life, death, resurrection, and continuing lordship. Where I have tried to use prayer, service, feeling, or discipline as wages, expose the bargain. Teach me to receive grace and then to obey freely. Let assurance turn my attention outward from endless self-measurement toward Christ.

## Confession and repentance

Holy God, I will not hide what you already know. I confess my sin without excuse. Give me sorrow that tells the truth and repentance that changes direction. Where I have harmed others, show me what repair is appropriate. Keep me from using shame as payment or forgiveness as an escape from consequences. Lead me back through Christ into honest obedience.

## Lament

God, I do not understand what has happened. I do not want to call evil good or pain small. Hear my complaint. Give me words when I have them and receive me when I do not. Protect me from false explanations and from despair. Give me the help I need through people, practical care, and your Spirit. Hold me while the question remains open.

## When prayer is unanswered

Father, I have asked for this and still ask. I do not know why the answer has not come as I hoped. Keep me from blaming weak faith, inventing hidden guilt, or pretending I no longer care. Give me sustaining grace for what remains. Make me willing to obey without calling the loss good. Keep hope alive in Christ.

## For a neighbor

Lord, show me the true good of the person before me. Keep me from using love as a name for approval, control, or self-display. Give me courage to help materially, speak truthfully, listen patiently, protect when necessary, and refuse vengeance. Teach me finite faithfulness rather than vague concern for everyone and concrete care for no one.

## For an enemy

God of justice and mercy, keep hatred from ruling me. I ask for the good of the one who opposes or has harmed me, including repentance and truth. Protect me from revenge. Give wisdom about distance, accountability, and safety. Do not let mercy become denial or justice become cruelty.

## For the church

Lord Jesus, shepherd your church. Give leaders courage, humility, accountability, and love. Protect the vulnerable. Expose hidden harm. Give members patience in ordinary conflict and courage in serious wrongdoing. Restore trust where truth and repentance make restoration possible. Lead those wounded by churches toward safe Christian community in your time.

## For witness

Father, make me ready to speak of Christ with gentleness, clarity, and courage. I cannot control another person's response. Make me faithful in truth and love, and work by your grace according to your wisdom. Keep me from manipulation, fear, and silence. Let my deeds adorn rather than replace the gospel I confess.

## For cross-cultural mission

God of all peoples, free your church from pride. Give missionaries linguistic patience, cultural humility, theological courage, and willingness to be corrected. Give local churches authority to lead faithfully under your Word. Protect money from becoming control. Heal wounds left by mission sin, and multiply reciprocal partnership across your global church.

## New creation hope

God who raises the dead, fix my hope beyond the limits of this age. When love feels unfinished, remind me that your work is not. Keep me faithful without making me frantic. Prepare me for the day when death, curse, sin, and fear are gone and your servants see your face. Until then, teach me to worship, obey, love, serve, and wait.
