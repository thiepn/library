A rule of life is a prudential structure for organizing recurring practices around Christian purposes. Scripture does not command every Christian to have a written rule, and no particular schedule is universally faithful.

## 1. Begin with responsibilities and constraints

List what God has actually placed in your life:

- household and caregiving;
- work or study;
- church;
- health and disability;
- sleep and bodily needs;
- financial realities;
- safety or persecution concerns;
- commuting and geography;
- existing ministry responsibilities.

Do not design a spiritual life for an imaginary person with unlimited private time.

## 2. Choose purposes before practices

Examples:

- receive Scripture attentively;
- pray honestly;
- participate in church;
- resist a particular sin;
- serve neighbors;
- practice rest;
- maintain relationships;
- support witness or mission.

A purpose can often be served by several practices.

## 3. Choose a small set of practices

Possible daily practices: short prayer, Scripture, memorization, examen-like reflection, household worship, intercession.

Possible weekly practices: gathered church, extended prayer, service, hospitality, rest, conversation with a mature believer.

Possible seasonal practices: fasting, retreat, focused study, financial review, digital reduction, mission discernment.

Treat these as options, not commands.

## 4. Build flexibility

Use ranges rather than perfectionistic rules where helpful. A parent with a newborn may need a minimum rhythm. A student on break may use longer study. A shift worker may rotate times. An oral learner may listen rather than read. A persecuted believer may need discreet practices.

The rule should serve love and faithfulness. If protecting the rule repeatedly harms responsibilities, revise the rule.

## 5. Review without scorekeeping

Every few weeks or months ask:

- What practice is serving its purpose?
- What has become mechanical?
- What responsibility is being neglected?
- What needs to become smaller or more sustainable?
- What season has changed?
- What fruit is visible at pattern level?

Do not convert the review into a salvation score.
