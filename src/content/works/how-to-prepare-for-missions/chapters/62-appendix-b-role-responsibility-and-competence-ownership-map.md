## Responsibility Map

For each major responsibility, record:

- main tasks;
- people affected;
- decisions owned;
- consequence of failure.

## Competence Ownership

For each capability, identify where primary responsibility belongs:

- individual;
- household;
- team;
- sending church;
- mission organization;
- local church or leader;
- specialist or external institution.

You may also find it useful to distinguish whether you must **own** the capability, merely **understand** enough to work responsibly around it, or simply **access** qualified help when needed.

Do not turn those labels into a score.

## Evidence

For each capability you personally own, ask:

> What demonstrates competence beyond interest, exposure, or self-confidence?

Possible evidence includes supervised performance, professional qualification, language assessment, repeated ministry feedback, portfolio work, examination, or observed responsibility.
