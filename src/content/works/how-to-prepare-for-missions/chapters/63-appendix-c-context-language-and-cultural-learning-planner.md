## Language Functions

For each relevant function, decide whether it is needed:

- pre-field;
- before independent responsibility;
- primarily through field learning;
- not required for this role.

Possible functions include everyday conversation, church participation, relationship building, professional work, Bible or theology discussion, teaching, counseling, reading, and writing.

## Culture-Learning Record

### Observation
What happened?

### Initial interpretation
What did I think it meant?

### Alternative explanations
What else might explain it?

### Local correction
Who helped interpret it?

### Revision
What changed in my understanding?

### Uncertainty
What remains unclear?

## Authority and Power Questions

- Who decides?
- Who controls resources?
- Who can veto?
- Who supervises me?
- Who can correct me?
- Whose expertise is assumed?
- Who bears the consequences of error?
- Which authority should become more locally rooted if that fits the ministry's purpose?
