## Scripture Translation Note

Unless otherwise indicated, Scripture quotations use the Berean Standard Bible (BSB). The book usually paraphrases biblical texts rather than reproducing them at length. Scripture references are presented for contextual theological argument rather than as decorative proof texts.

## Note on Terminology and Current Data

Mission terminology varies among churches, researchers, and mission organizations. Terms such as *missionary*, *unreached*, *frontier*, *member care*, and *safeguarding* are therefore used with defined rather than assumed meanings. Strategic mission categories are not treated as biblical categories or as descriptions of every individual within a population.

Some organizational policies, laws, immigration rules, professional standards, security conditions, and technology capabilities change. Where these affect an actual assignment, verify current requirements through official or qualified sources. The central argument of this book rests on more durable questions: discipleship, responsibility, competence, church, context, accountability, and continued learning.
