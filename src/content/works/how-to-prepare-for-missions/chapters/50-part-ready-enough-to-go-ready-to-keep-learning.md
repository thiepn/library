You can now construct a preparation list far longer than the one you began with.

That would defeat the purpose of the book.

The final task is not to add more preparation.

It is to classify what remains.

Some gaps should delay departure. Some need continued development but do not make the assignment irresponsible. Some belong to other people or systems. Some should be learned after arrival. Some are merely useful extras.

Part V turns preparation into judgment.
