**Assignment** - The actual combination of role, context, people, team or organization, household reality, and responsibility a worker may enter.

**Blocker** - A gap serious enough that deployment should ordinarily wait unless the gap or assignment changes.

**Calling** - A term that may refer to discipleship, vocation, ministry responsibility, specific assignment, or subjective guidance. The book distinguishes these rather than treating them as one experience.

**Competence** - Demonstrated ability adequate for a particular responsibility.

**Context hypothesis** - A provisional account of where a defined responsibility may genuinely fit.

**Contextualization** - Faithful communication and embodiment of Christian truth within a particular setting without equating Christianity with the missionary's culture.

**Cross-cultural mission** - Christian service involving substantial engagement across linguistic, cultural, ethnic, national, or other significant social boundaries.

**Development need** - A real area for growth that does not automatically prevent deployment.

**Field learning** - Competence intentionally developed substantially through actual life and service within the receiving context.

**Frontier people group** - A modern strategic research category whose exact definition depends on the methodology being used.

**Local Christian agency** - Meaningful participation and decision authority exercised by Christians rooted in the receiving context.

**Member care** - Pastoral, relational, psychological, practical, and organizational care intended to support missionaries and households.

**Mission agency** - An organization involved in preparing, sending, supporting, or supervising missionary workers.

**Missionary** - A practical modern category for a Christian carrying defined missionary responsibility; not a higher class of Christian.

**Optional enhancement** - Useful preparation that is not necessary for responsible performance of the assignment.

**Readiness** - Sufficient preparation for a defined responsibility rather than completion of all possible development.

**Responsibility** - What a person or system is genuinely accountable to perform or steward well.

**Role hypothesis** - A provisional but concrete description of the kind of work a prospective missionary may be preparing to carry.

**Safeguarding** - Structures and practices intended to prevent and respond appropriately to abuse or exploitation of children or vulnerable people.

**Team/system responsibility** - An essential capability or obligation that properly belongs outside the individual worker.

**Unreached people group** - A contemporary strategic classification whose threshold depends on the research system being used and should not be treated as biblical ontology.
