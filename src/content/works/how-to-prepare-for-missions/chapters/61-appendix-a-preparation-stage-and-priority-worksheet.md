## Locate Your Stage

### Stage 1 - Serious Interest
I am genuinely considering long-term cross-cultural mission, but no clear role or context exists yet.

### Stage 2 - Active Exploration
I am discussing missions with my church and investigating plausible roles.

### Stage 3 - Role Forming
One or more roles are becoming plausible.

### Stage 4 - Assignment Forming
Role, context, organization or team, and household implications are increasingly concrete.

### Stage 5 - Pre-Deployment
A real assignment exists and remaining blockers are being resolved.

## Classify Your Current Preparation

For each relevant preparation item, choose one:

- NOW
- MAINTAIN
- NEXT
- LATER
- FIELD
- NOT REQUIRED FOR THIS ROLE

Consider character, church involvement, ministry practice, education, professional skill, language, cultural learning, team preparation, household, finance, health, organization, and risk.

For each item, write why you gave it that classification and who should review the judgment.

## Find the Bottleneck

Complete:

> If I made substantial progress in only one area over the next six months, the area most likely to improve the quality of my missionary decision would be: __________.

Why?

What evidence supports that conclusion?

Who might reasonably disagree?
