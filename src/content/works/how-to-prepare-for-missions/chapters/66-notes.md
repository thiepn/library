## Introduction

1. Gospel Mobilization, “The Launch Survey,” 2025. The survey was conducted in the first quarter of 2025 with 2,428 respondents invited through fourteen email lists. It is used here as North American, mission-interested evidence rather than as a global census.

## Chapter 8

1. Pooja K. Agarwal, Ludmila D. Nunes, and Janell R. Blunt, “Retrieval Practice Consistently Benefits Student Learning: A Systematic Review of Applied Research in Schools and Classrooms,” *Educational Psychology Review* 33 (2021): 1409-1453, <https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09595-9>.
2. Shana K. Carpenter, Steven C. Pan, and Andrew C. Butler, “The Science of Effective Learning with Spacing and Retrieval Practice,” *Nature Reviews Psychology* 1 (2022): 496-511, <https://doi.org/10.1038/s44159-022-00089-1>.
3. Su Kyung Kim and Stuart Webb, “The Effects of Spaced Practice on Second Language Learning: A Meta-Analysis,” *Language Learning* 72 (2022): 269-319, <https://doi.org/10.1111/lang.12479>. The meta-analysis synthesized 48 experiments involving 3,411 participants.
4. Benedikt Wisniewski, Klaus Zierer, and John Hattie, “The Power of Feedback Revisited: A Meta-Analysis of Educational Feedback Research,” *Frontiers in Psychology* 10 (2020): 3087, <https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2019.03087>. The meta-analysis included 435 studies, 994 effects, and more than 61,000 learners.
5. Susan M. Barnett and Stephen J. Ceci, “When and Where Do We Apply What We Learn? A Taxonomy for Far Transfer,” *Psychological Bulletin* 128, no. 4 (2002): 612-637, <https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.128.4.612>.

## Chapter 12

1. ACTFL, *ACTFL Proficiency Guidelines 2024* (Alexandria, VA: ACTFL, 2024). The book refers only to the framework at a descriptive level; it does not reproduce ACTFL descriptors or tables.
2. Council of Europe, *Common European Framework of Reference for Languages: Learning, Teaching, Assessment - Companion Volume* (Strasbourg: Council of Europe Publishing, 2020), ISBN 978-92-871-8621-8.

## Chapter 16

1. Emma Marchal Jones, Marnie Reed, Jens Gaab, and Yoon Phaik Ooi, “Adjustment in Third Culture Kids: A Systematic Review of Literature,” *Frontiers in Psychology* 13 (2022): 939044, <https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2022.939044>. Only fourteen studies met the review’s eligibility criteria, so the chapter deliberately avoids universal claims about missionary-child outcomes.

## Chapter 17

1. Rob Hay, Valerie Lim, and Detlef Bloecher, eds., *Worth Keeping: Global Perspectives on Best Practice in Missionary Retention* (Pasadena, CA: William Carey Library, 2007). ReMAP II material is used as historical comparative evidence, not as current global prevalence data.
