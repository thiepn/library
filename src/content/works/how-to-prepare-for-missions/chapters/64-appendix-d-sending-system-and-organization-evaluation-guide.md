This guide evaluates functions, not one corporate form. A small church network and a large mission organization may structure them differently. What matters is whether the necessary responsibilities are genuinely covered.

## Doctrine

- What does the organization believe?
- Which doctrinal issues affect placement?
- How are disagreements handled?

## Role

- Is the role written clearly?
- Who can change it?
- What expectations are implicit?

## Authority

- Who supervises?
- Who evaluates the supervisor?
- What appeal exists?

## Grievance

- Is there a route outside the normal reporting chain?
- How is retaliation handled?
- What if senior leadership is implicated?

## Safeguarding

- What prevention and reporting policies exist?
- Who receives reports?
- What external reporting is legally required in the relevant jurisdiction?
- How are investigation and pastoral care kept appropriately distinct?

Verify actual legal requirements through current official or qualified professional sources.

## Member Care

- Who provides care?
- What is confidential?
- Can outside professional care be used?
- Does counseling information affect employment or placement?

## Household

- How are spouses, children, and single workers supported?
- Can family members seek help independently?
- What educational or medical support is realistically available?

## Finance

- How does support work?
- What administrative costs exist?
- What happens during support shortfall?
- What insurance or emergency resources exist?

## Crisis

How does the organization respond to severe illness, evacuation, political crisis, arrest, abuse allegation, sudden departure, or other plausible emergencies?

## Locally Rooted Christian Authority

- Who controls strategy?
- How are local churches involved?
- Who controls property and resources?
- Who evaluates expatriate workers?

## Exit

- Can workers leave safely?
- How are transfers handled?
- What re-entry support exists?

### Final diagnostic question

> Would this system still appear trustworthy if the person accused of wrongdoing were one of its most powerful leaders?
