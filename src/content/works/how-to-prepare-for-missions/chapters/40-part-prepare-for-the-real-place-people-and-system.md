Broad preparation has now reached its limit.

You have moved from missionary interest toward a plausible role. You have identified responsibilities, considered where required competence needs to reside, and begun connecting that responsibility to a real context.

Now specificity is useful.

The next questions cannot be answered well for missionaries in general. They depend on the assignment.

Which language functions do you need? How will you learn a culture without pretending to master it? Who will hold authority? What kind of team are you entering? Who else in your household bears the consequences? Can the work be sustained? Is the organization trustworthy? What will the assignment actually cost?

Part IV takes those questions seriously because the assignment is finally specific enough for them to matter.
