Missionary preparation does not begin with a competency list. It begins with the kind of Christian life those competencies are meant to serve.

This Part establishes four foundations for everything that follows: missionary service does not create a higher class of Christian; Scripture governs missionary preparation without prescribing one universal modern training system; God's guidance should be taken seriously without requiring every missionary to experience the same kind of special call; and discernment should normally take place within the life of Christ's church rather than as a private project.

Only after those questions are in order does it make sense to ask what you should start building.
