## Remaining Gaps

For each unresolved issue, classify it provisionally as:

- BLOCKER
- DEVELOPMENT
- TEAM/SYSTEM
- FIELD
- OPTIONAL

Then record why, who is competent to judge, and what new evidence could change the classification.

Do not score or average the categories.

## Decision

### GO
Why is the assignment sufficiently ready?

### WAIT
What specific blocker requires attention, and when will it be reviewed?

### CHANGE
What should change: role, context, organization, timing, team, funding model, or another feature?

### DECLINE
Why is this opportunity not responsible?

## Decision Participants

Record the appropriate participants for your actual assignment:

- candidate;
- spouse or household;
- church;
- mission organization;
- team;
- locally rooted Christian leader;
- professional reviewer;
- other relevant person.

Record meaningful disagreements rather than erasing them.

## First-Term Learning Plan

### Language
What functions need concentrated development?

### Local relationships
Whom do you need to know before trying to change anything?

### Church integration
Where will you worship, belong, listen, and serve?

### Cultural questions
Which assumptions need testing?

### Team integration
Which roles and expectations need clarification?

### Role calibration
Which parts of the role should begin immediately, and which should wait?

### Household priorities
What needs stabilization?

### Spiritual life
Which ordinary means of grace and Christian relationships need protection?

### Supervision
Who reviews the first season?

### Review date
When will the assignment hypothesis be revisited?
